Mission briefing

What is this event?

A live-action, campus-wide version of Among Us. You and 199 others walk around real task stations, impostors secretly tag crewmates, and everyone argues in emergency meetings until someone gets ejected.

Crewmates

Walk the campus completing real tasks, watch who acts suspicious, report tagged players and vote out the impostors.

Impostors

Blend in, fake tasks, tag crewmates, trigger sabotages and lie with a straight face. Don't get voted out!

Prizes & glory

Trophies and goodies for the top crew and best impostor, plus a lifetime of bragging rights.

The plan

What will happen

The event runs in four stages across the day. Just bring your college ID and comfortable shoes.

Check-in

10:00 AM. Collect your wristband, get your secret role card and hear the safety briefing.

Qualifiers

10 rounds of 20 players each roam the campus zones. Top survivors from every round advance.

Semi-finals

Two tense rounds with fewer tasks, more sabotage and louder arguments.

The Finale

The final round plays out in the main arena with everyone watching. Winners are announced on stage.

Your missions

Tasks you'll do

Real tasks at real stations. Volunteers run each task zone across the campus and tick you off when you finish.

Crewmate tasks

🔌 Fix Wiring

Match the coloured wires on a panel board to their matching ends before the timer runs out.

💾 Download Data

Scan the QR code at one station, then find and scan the upload station on the other side of campus.

⛽ Fuel Engines

Carry a cup of water between two stations without spilling and fill the tank to the marked line.

💳 Swipe Card

Swipe your task card through the reader at just the right speed. Too fast or too slow and you retry.

🧩 Solve the Riddle

Crack a campus puzzle or trivia question to open the lock box and claim your task stamp.

🗑️ Clean the Filter

Sort coloured balls into the correct bins within the time limit to clear the vents.

Impostors, sabotage & meetings

🔪 The Tag

Impostors gently tap a crewmate's shoulder and show their secret card. The tagged player sits down on the spot as a "body" until someone reports them. Then they become a ghost who can still finish tasks.

🚨 Sabotage

Volunteers announce a sabotage such as Reactor Meltdown. Two crewmates must scan two far-apart stations at the same time within 2 minutes, or the crew loses the round.

🗳️ Emergency Meeting

Report a body or hit the button at the Main Auditorium table. Everyone gathers, debates for 2 minutes and votes. The most-voted player is ejected.

How to win: crewmates win by finishing all tasks or ejecting every impostor. Impostors win when they equal the number of crewmates, or when a sabotage goes unfixed.
